Simple by design

A small switch. A smarter routine.

StepGlow combines a familiar light-switch idea with helpful features that make everyday habits easier.

☼

Find it in the dark

A soft illuminated button helps you locate the switch at night without needing to search along the wall.

◉

Reminder concept

A proposed room-occupancy sensor could trigger a gentle flash when lights appear to be left on in an empty room.

↯

Build better habits

Make it easier to remember lights and encourage more thoughtful electricity use at home or school.

Interactive demo

Your energy habits, at a glance.

The switch changes color based on how well you're saving power. Try each setting, then simulate leaving the room with the light on.

Green · Great job!

You're saving power by switching lights off when you don't need them.

Choose your energy habit

Green = doing great. Yellow = room to improve. Red = lights are being wasted. In a real product, sensors would estimate the state; this is a website demonstration.
